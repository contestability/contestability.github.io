Description

The increasing complexity of decision-making processes across domains has led to the demand for a structured approach to contest decisions. With AI systems playing an increasingly significant role in decision making, there is an imperative need for a mechanism that allows humans to engage with these systems in a human-centric way that is both robust and understandable, in order to challenge and verify their alignment with regulatory and ethical standards.
